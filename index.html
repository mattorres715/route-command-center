Route Command Center

Add stops

One stop per line: Name, Street, City, ZIP, Notes, Closes, Lat, Lng (last three optional). A header row also works, and may include an optional color column (red, orange, yellow, green, blue or purple).

Route

Business priorities

Teach one from any stop card with “Save to business list”.

Business suggestions

Free. Checks each stop's location for a named business nearby and asks you on the stop card ("Looks like …? Yes / Not this"). Nothing is added to the map, and nothing changes until you tap Yes. Hours come from OpenStreetMap and may be out of date. Only stop locations are sent, never names.

Saved locations


Separate from the business list. When you pin a stop on the map or paste coordinates, that exact address (same house number, street and ZIP) uses your pin every time after.

Color labels

To tag a stop, open it and tap “✎ Edit address, priority, hours, notes & color”, then pick a color. Colors are only for organizing your van — they do not change priority, the route, or the business list. Rename them here if you want, for example Blue = “Business, closes late”.

Backup

Shares businesses, closing times, notes and saved pins only — no customer stops. Your coworker loads it with Restore backup. Moving to another device: copy the backup text, send it to yourself, then paste it below on the other device.
